# Tarot pack art

The 54 pictures are the **Rider–Waite–Smith tarot** (1909), drawn by Pamela Colman Smith for A. E. Waite and
published by William Rider & Son. Scans from Wikimedia Commons (`Cups01.jpg` … `Wands14.jpg`,
`RWS Tarot 00 Fool.jpg`, `RWS Tarot 01 Magician.jpg`), trimmed to the cards' black frame line, lightly cleaned of paper
grain and saved as 280px-wide WebP by `tools/make-tarot.py`.

How the tarot maps onto Bankroll's deck:

| Tarot | Bankroll |
|---|---|
| Cups / Pentacles / Wands / Swords | ♥ / ♦ / ♣ / ♠ |
| Ace … Ten | A … 10 |
| Page / Queen / King | J / Q / K (Knights are not used) |
| The Fool / The Magician | Red Joker / Black Joker |

File names: suit letter + rank (`H01` … `S13`), `JR`, `JB`.

**Copyright.** Pamela Colman Smith died in 1951, so the 1909 art is in the public domain in the UK, the EU and every
country with a term of life + 70 years or less (since 1 January 2022), and Wikimedia Commons hosts it as public domain.
In the **United States its status is disputed**: U.S. Games Systems, which publishes the deck there, says it holds
rights. Later recoloured editions (1971 onwards) are separate and are not used here. If the US question matters for a
store or portal, swap this pack's pictures out or limit it by region.
