# Classic court card art

The 12 court images (J, Q, K of each suit) are the framed centre panels of the court cards in
**Vector Playing Cards** by Byron Knoll, taken from https://github.com/notpeter/Vector-Playing-Cards
(`cards-svg/`). The two suit pips drawn inside each panel were removed (the game's own corner index shows
rank and suit), then the panel was rendered without its white card background and frame line, and saved as 240px-wide WebP with
transparency, so the art sits straight on the game's card.

Licence: public domain (or WTFPL where public domain is not recognised), as stated in that
repository's README. The original images were released into the public domain by Byron Knoll on
Google Code as vector-playing-cards.
